02 · Conviction

What I Believe

Five working principles. Not a manifesto carved in stone—an operating system tested through markets, companies, and cycles.

Working thesis · 2026Ideas earn conviction.

Belief without evidence is branding. Evidence without action is trivia.

Five convictions

Evidence over slogans
01 / TIME

Discipline compounds longer than narratives last.

Markets reward patience unevenly. The durable edge is a process that survives both euphoria and drawdown.

02 / MONEY

Bitcoin is a monetary system, not only a trade.

Price is the loudest signal, but monetary credibility is the deeper product.

03 / OPENNESS

Open financial infrastructure outlives closed distribution.

Permissionless rails turn access into a primitive, not a business favour.

04 / PRIVACY

Confidentiality is required for serious AI adoption.

Institutions cannot place their most valuable data and decisions inside systems they cannot control.

05 / SYSTEMS

Infrastructure and incentives matter more than interfaces.

Beautiful surfaces can accelerate adoption. They cannot repair broken coordination underneath.

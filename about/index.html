About Rezo Shmertz

Building conviction through cycles.

Rezo Shmertz is a founder, investor and researcher working across crypto markets, financial infrastructure and confidential AI. He is a co-founder and Managing Partner of BR Capital, with a professional background spanning venture investment, algorithmic trading and institutional participation in decentralized finance.

His company-building work includes co-founding Super Protocol and T-Digital. Super Protocol develops confidential computing infrastructure for AI, focused on protecting data and computations during execution. T-Digital builds software for the financial industry. These activities connect his investment work with the technical and operational challenges of building financial and computing infrastructure.

Alongside building and investing, Rezo publishes research and commentary on Bitcoin, decentralized finance, stablecoins and AI. His writing and BR Labs podcasts examine market structure, trading infrastructure and incentives. He also participates in industry panels on digital assets and financial technology, including discussions of stablecoin adoption and institutional DeFi.

About this snapshot

Price and drawdown each carry their own observation date. Cycle day counts elapsed days from the configured reference date of 6 Oct 2025. The recorded phase combines MVRV, NUPL, ahr999 and long-term holder supply; it is not determined by cycle day alone.

While this page is visible it checks the tracker source periodically. A successful refresh updates the snapshot metadata; it does not necessarily mean every market observation is new.

Intent-based trading and execution

Intent-based trading separates a user's desired outcome from the route used to achieve it. This episode looks at the solvers competing to provide that execution, with BRRRolver as an operating example. Auctions, pricing and available capital make the discussion relevant to both DeFi users and the teams building trading infrastructure.

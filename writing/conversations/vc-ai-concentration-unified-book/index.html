AI investing and trading infrastructure

This episode brings together two areas of BR Labs' work: assessing companies in a fast-changing AI market and operating across crypto trading venues. The investment discussion focuses on founder adaptability and product durability. The desk discussion describes a unified CeFi and DeFi book as a goal, not a completed integration.

Rezo Shmertz discusses tokenized equities as a destination for market flows and compares Robinhood's distribution with other routes into tokenized stocks. Tokenized equities and market distribution

Tokenized equities connect stock-market exposure with crypto trading infrastructure. This episode considers how distribution, account sizes and trading outside stock-market hours shape their use. It also keeps the question of what a token represents separate from how many people can access it.

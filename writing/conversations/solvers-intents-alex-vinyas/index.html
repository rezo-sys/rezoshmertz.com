Episode topics

The BR Labs guest episode traces CoW Swap's origins within Gnosis and the use of batch auctions to match trading intentions. Alex Vinyas discusses solver competition, BR Capital's investment history with CoW and how intent-based execution can support software acting on a user's instructions.

Solvers and intent-based execution

An intent specifies the outcome a user wants and the limits an execution must respect. Solvers compete to find a route that satisfies those instructions. The episode explores how this division of responsibilities developed at CoW Swap and why it matters as more financial activity is initiated by software.

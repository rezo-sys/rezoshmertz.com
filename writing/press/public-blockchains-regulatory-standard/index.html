Public Blockchains Remain the Regulatory Standard

Corporate blockchain launches can satisfy particular institutional constraints without replacing the neutral settlement and coordination properties of public networks.

Quoted analysis · Rezo Shmertz · September 5, 2025

Read the original CryptoSlate coverage ↗

Context

CryptoSlate examined the rise of corporate layer-one networks and the regulatory arguments used to support them. Rezo Shmertz was quoted on the difference between compliant enterprise infrastructure and neutral public protocols.

Why the distinction matters

A controlled network can make governance and compliance easier for a particular institution. A public blockchain serves a different function: open access, shared settlement, composability, and credible neutrality across participants that do not share an owner.

The existence of corporate chains therefore does not establish that open networks have failed. It shows that institutions are experimenting with different points on the control-to-neutrality spectrum.

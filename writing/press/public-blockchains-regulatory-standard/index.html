Corporate L1s and regulatory arbitrage

CryptoSlate reports on a debate about public and corporate blockchains, including Rezo Shmertz's contrasting view on corporate L1s and regulatory arbitrage.

Coverage

CryptoSlate reported on a debate between Jake Chervinsky and Rezo Shmertz over corporate layer-one blockchains. Rezo argued that companies can use these networks to respond to regulatory uncertainty, with corporate chains serving institutional needs alongside neutral public protocols.

Public and corporate blockchain infrastructure

The coverage concerns a distinction between neutral public protocols and networks developed around corporate needs. Rezo Shmertz's contribution addresses how corporate layer-one blockchains might respond to regulatory uncertainty. That position is part of the debate reported by CryptoSlate, not the conclusion of every participant.

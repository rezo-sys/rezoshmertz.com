About this writing archive

This archive brings together Rezo Shmertz’s essays on money, markets and technology. Topics include Bitcoin and stablecoins, decentralized finance (DeFi), market infrastructure and artificial intelligence. Recurring questions concern how financial systems earn trust, how incentives shape decisions and where market advantages come from.

Each entry includes an excerpt and a link to the original publication. Read across the years to follow an argument over time, or choose a subject from the titles above. Older pieces remain dated so their arguments can be read in the context in which they were written.

For data alongside the essays, explore the Bitcoin Cycle Tracker. Interviews, podcasts and public appearances are collected separately in Media.

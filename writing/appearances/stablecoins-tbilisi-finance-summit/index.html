Appearance · Tbilisi Finance Summit · Roundtable

What's Next for Stablecoins in the Region

A regional discussion about how regulation, product design, payments, and financial infrastructure shape the next phase of stablecoin adoption.

Roundtable participant · Rezo Shmertz · October 22, 2025

Open the official summit session ↗

Session focus

The roundtable examined implications of the US GENIUS Act, the product roadmaps forming around regulated stablecoins, and the conditions required for useful regional adoption.

  • How regulation changes issuer and distributor incentives
  • Where compliance belongs in the product stack
  • How stablecoins interact with banking and payment rails
  • Which regional use cases can create durable demand

Rezo's perspective

Rezo Shmertz participated as a founder, investor, and digital-markets operator. The discussion connected policy with product reality: stablecoins become infrastructure when users can access them through trustworthy rails and when institutions can understand the operational and regulatory responsibility behind them.
